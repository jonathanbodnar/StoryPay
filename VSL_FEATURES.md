# StoryVenue — Full Feature → Outcome Reference (VSL Source Doc)

> Purpose: a complete, current inventory of what StoryVenue actually does — every feature, on both the **venue side** and the **bride/couple side**, paired with the outcome/benefit it produces. Pure feature → outcome. No competitive positioning, no objection handling, no limitations.

---

## PART 1 — VENUE SIDE

### 1. Lead Inbox

**What it does:** Every inquiry — from the venue's directory listing, pricing guide download, embedded lead form, or Lead Link™ — lands in a single Lead Inbox the moment it's submitted. New leads trigger a real-time badge/notification.

**Outcome:** The owner never has to check five different places (website form, directory, social bio link) to see who inquired. Every lead is visible the second it arrives.

---

### 2. Speed to Lead System™ (automated lead follow-up)

**What it does:** A five-phase automation engine that follows a lead from first inquiry all the way through the first wedding anniversary, with a master on/off switch and per-phase controls:

- **Phase 1 — Inquiry → Guide Delivered:** The instant a bride submits the listing form, she's sent the venue's pricing guide by email and/or SMS automatically, using the venue's own custom-written copy.
- **Phase 2 — Guide Delivered → 14-Day Sequence:** A run of SMS nurture touch-points over two weeks (fully editable per-message) that keeps the lead warm until she replies or books a tour.
- **Phase 3 — Booked Tour → Toured:** Fires the moment a lead moves into the "Tour Booked" pipeline stage — sends everything she needs before the visit (directions, what to expect, reminders).
- **Phase 4 — Wedding Day → Welcomed:** Fires when a lead moves to "Wedding Booked" — a warm welcome sequence with what happens next.
- **Anniversary → Celebrated:** A single automatic email exactly one year after the wedding date — a warm, low-effort touch that keeps referrals and reviews coming long after the event.

Every phase's message copy is fully editable, supports merge tags (first name, venue name, owner name, appointment date/time, venue address, pricing guide link), and can be tested by sending to yourself before going live. A live "leads active in this stage" counter shows exactly how many people are sitting in each step in real time.

**Outcome:** Every single lead gets a response within minutes of inquiring — even at 2am — and continues to be nurtured automatically for weeks without the owner touching a keyboard. Tours get booked, deposits get collected, and past couples get a reason to refer friends — all on autopilot.

---

### 3. AI Concierge (add-on)

**What it does:** A personal AI assistant, layered on top of the 14-day sequence, that keeps texting quiet leads (in the venue's own voice) roughly every 4–5 days for up to 60 days after a lead goes quiet — respecting 9am–8pm send hours so nothing lands at an odd hour. It reads inbound replies, determines intent (interested, booked elsewhere, not interested, wants a human), stops instantly and hands the conversation off to the owner the moment a real reply needs a real person, and automatically exhausts/opts a lead out once the 60-day window closes. A performance dashboard shows messages sent, reply rate, hand-offs, and opt-outs against a daily send cap, and a live leads monitor lets staff pause, resume, snooze, force-send, or preview a draft message for any individual lead before it goes out.

**Outcome:** Leads who go quiet don't just fall off — they keep getting personalized, natural-sounding follow-up for two full months, with full visibility and manual override on every single conversation. The owner only steps in when a lead is actually ready to talk, instead of manually chasing cold texts.

---

### 4. Conversations (unified inbox)

**What it does:** Every SMS and email conversation with every lead and client lives in one threaded inbox, visible to the whole team, with unread badges and instant real-time updates.

**Outcome:** No more digging through separate text threads, email accounts, or "did anyone reply to that lead?" — one place shows every conversation with every person.

---

### 5. Venue Concierge (white-glove support, add-on)

**What it does:** A direct messaging channel straight to the StoryVenue team, with a visible "typically replies in X" response-time expectation, for venues that want hands-on help running the platform.

**Outcome:** Owners get real help from a real person without hunting for a support email or waiting on hold — questions get answered inside the same dashboard they already work in.

---

### 6. Wedding Planner (venue side)

**What it does:** Once a booked couple connects their own Wedding Planner account to the venue (either the venue invites them, or the couple requests the connection and the venue approves), the venue gets:
- A live roster of every connected couple, with guest RSVP counts (attending / pending / total) visible at a glance.
- The couple's full guest list, RSVP status, and per-guest meal/allergy choices — the same data the couple manages on her end, always current.
- A shared room/seating layout canvas — the venue and the couple can both view (and, depending on settings, edit) the actual floor plan, table placement, and seating chart together, with conflict-safe saving so simultaneous edits never overwrite each other.
- A one-click link from any connected couple's card straight to that couple's full contact profile — and, conversely, a Wedding Planner status badge and full guest/seating detail card embedded right inside that couple's contact profile, so a venue never has to leave the contact to see her wedding details.
- Wedding date and guest count are automatically kept in sync between the couple's own profile and the venue's contact record the moment either side updates it, so the two can never show conflicting information.
- Search-and-invite: venues can search their own existing contacts by name, phone, or email to send a Wedding Planner invite, or create a brand-new contact on the spot if the couple isn't in the system yet.
- Automatic system emails: the couple gets a branded invite email when a venue invites her to connect; the venue owner gets a notification email the moment she accepts.

**Outcome:** The venue always has an up-to-the-minute view of every wedding's guest count, RSVPs, meal counts, and table assignments — sourced directly from the couple, with zero double data entry and zero risk of the venue's copy drifting out of sync with the couple's.

---

### 7. Contacts & CRM

**What it does:** A centralized client database that auto-populates from proposals, forms, calendar bookings, Wedding Planner connections, and manual entry. Every interaction — proposals, payments, messages, tags, AI conversations, Wedding Planner guest/seating data — is linked to the one contact record. Supports CSV import/export and duplicate merging.

**Outcome:** One source of truth per client. When a bride calls asking about her balance, her guest count, or anything else, the answer is a single click away instead of a hunt across four different tools.

---

### 8. Calendar & Booking

**What it does:** A full calendar with space-based booking (ballroom, garden, rooftop — each tracked independently), recurring events, conflict detection, Google Calendar sync, and automated appointment reminders to clients. Calendly connection syncs upcoming scheduled events straight in.

**Outcome:** No double-bookings across multiple event spaces, and clients show up prepared because they got automatic reminders — without the owner manually re-checking a spreadsheet.

### 9. Payments Suite

**What it does:**
- **Proposals & Invoices:** Branded, mobile-friendly proposal pages built from the venue's own offerings, sent as one shareable link; draft or sent status, resend, delete drafts, and a "record payment" option for anything collected outside the platform (cash/check/Venmo).
- **Proposal templates:** Reusable templates so a new proposal takes seconds, not a rewrite each time.
- **E-signatures:** Legally binding signature capture (ESIGN/UETA-compliant, tamper-evident document hashing, signer IP and consent recording) drawn right on the client's phone, tablet, or desktop.
- **Payments:** Full payment, deposit + balance, monthly installment plans, and recurring subscriptions — credit card and ACH, all inside the same flow.
- **Coupons:** Percent- or fixed-amount discount codes with optional redemption caps.
- **Installments & Subscriptions management:** Dedicated schedule views (client, total, "X of Y payments" completed, per-payment amount, status) to track every payment plan and recurring charge in flight, plus automated reminders on overdue installments.
- **Offerings (Packages):** One catalog covers everything sellable — simple **Items** (name, price, unit, one-time/weekly/monthly recurrence, unlimited or limited inventory) and **Bundles** (multiple items combined, optional seasonal label and valid date range, minimum-subtotal rules, per-line price overrides, and an attachable default e-signature contract that auto-loads the moment the bundle is added to a proposal).
- **Transactions:** A full charge/payment ledger with a detail view (charge/transaction/session IDs, full context for installment/subscription charges) and one-click full or partial refunds directly from the transaction.
- **Accounting Export:** A date-ranged CSV export of paid proposals and refunds (refunds recorded as negative amounts), formatted to hand straight to a bookkeeper.

**Outcome:** A couple can review a branded proposal, sign it, and pay a deposit — in full or on a plan — from her phone in one sitting, and the venue sees the money and the signed contract land in the same dashboard instantly, with every package, discount, and payment plan pulling from one consistent catalog.

---

### 10. Financial Reports

**What it does:** Seven built-in, date-filtered reports, each exportable (CSV/Excel/PDF):
1. **Revenue Report** — every paid transaction with customer, amount, and payment type.
2. **Proposals Report** — full proposal history, status, amounts, dates.
3. **Contact Summary** — unique contacts ranked by total spend, proposal count, and last activity.
4. **Accounts Receivable Aging** — outstanding unpaid proposals grouped by how long they've been open.
5. **Payment Method Breakdown** — conversion rates and revenue by payment type (full/installment/subscription).
6. **Refunds Report** — every refunded transaction with original amount and date.
7. **Bank Reconciliation** — matches gross payments to what actually deposits, net of processing fees and refunds — built to hand straight to an accountant.

Each report is date-range filterable (defaulting to year-to-date), previewed in-app with summary stat pills, and exportable as CSV, Excel, or PDF. An eighth, closely related tool — **Accounting Export** — sits alongside the seven reports as a dedicated CSV export of paid proposals and refunds formatted specifically for handoff to a bookkeeper.

**Outcome:** Real financial visibility without exporting to QuickBooks or building spreadsheet formulas — an owner can see exactly what's coming in, what's overdue, and what's actually hitting the bank, in a format their bookkeeper can use immediately.

### 11. Marketing Suite

**What it does:**
- **Analytics** — one dashboard rolling up template/campaign/workflow counts and active workflows, form count and suppressions, trigger-link clicks, form submissions (7-day vs. all-time), a full email deliverability funnel (sent, opened, bounced, unsubscribed, spam reports — every card clickable to drill into the exact contact list behind it), a form-submission breakdown by individual form, and CRM pipeline value, bookings this month vs. goal, and top lost-deal reasons.
- **Email Campaigns** — a Flodesk-style visual email design editor with venue branding, merge variables, and built-in CAN-SPAM/GDPR compliance; pick an audience, preview, schedule or send, and track each campaign's live status (draft/scheduled/sending/sent/failed) with drafts staying editable and resendable.
- **Audiences** — save reusable, filterable segments (by tag, pipeline stage, trigger-link click, or "all leads") with a live recipient-count preview; audiences are referenced by name across every campaign, so updating one segment updates every campaign that uses it.
- **Form Builder** — a drag-and-drop embeddable lead-capture form builder (text fields, file uploads, checkbox groups, addresses) that publishes to an iframe embed for the venue's own website, writes submissions straight into the CRM, and auto-triggers Speed to Lead follow-up, with spam rate-limiting built in.
- **Workflows/Automations** — a visual, canvas-based automation builder: triggers (new lead, form submitted, tag added, pipeline stage changed, trigger-link click, wedding-date follow-up, proposal signed/paid, email opened, etc.) paired with actions (send email, send SMS, add tag, change pipeline stage, notify owner, wait/delay), with each workflow showing a clear draft/active/paused status.
- **Media library** — centralized storage for venue photos/assets used across listings, proposals, and campaigns.

**Outcome:** A venue gets one screen that answers "is my marketing working" — deliverability health, funnel counts, and pipeline dollars, with drill-down to the real people behind every number — and can build a follow-up or promotional sequence once (a new inquiry triggers a welcome email, a signed proposal triggers onboarding, an overdue payment triggers a reminder) that runs forever with no manual work.

**Outcome:** Instant visual clarity on every lead and exactly where they stand — nothing falls through the cracks, and high-value leads get priority attention.

**Outcome:** Leads are automatically categorized by behavior and lifecycle stage with zero manual tagging — the owner filters by any tag combination to find exactly who needs attention.

### 15. Bride Booking System™ (Directory & Discovery Suite)

- **Venue Listing editor** — one screen (autosaving as you type) controls the entire public listing: name and URL slug, venue type, indoor/outdoor, contact email and phone, full location with address autocomplete and map, social links, an FAQ accordion, min/max guest capacity, price range, a long-form description and availability notes, feature/amenity chips (ceremony site, bridal suite, in-house catering, etc.), cover and gallery photos, and a one-click publish/unpublish toggle.
- **Reviews** — two sources feeding the same public listing: StoryVenue-native reviews (owner-added or couple-submitted, with star rating, title, body, wedding date, and publish/hide moderation, plus an embeddable widget for the venue's own website), and a direct Google Reviews sync (connected via the venue's Google Business Profile) that auto-refreshes every 24 hours and shows the live rating, count, and recent review previews with a link back to Google.
- **Lead Link™** — a single "link in bio" landing page (shown live in an on-screen iPhone-mockup preview) that auto-populates with the venue's listing, a "Download Pricing & Availability Guide" button, connected social handles, and any number of additional custom buttons (booking calendar, video tour, menu, etc.) with an icon picker.
- **Pricing Guide builder** — generates a fully branded, multi-page PDF automatically: cover, welcome note, photo gallery, about page, per-space pages, accommodations, packages/pricing (with AI-suggested included items), venue features, testimonials, FAQ, and a Save-the-Date/contact page — with AI-assisted copywriting per section, auto-fill from the existing listing data, a live preview, one-click download, and a copy-paste embed snippet (with UTM/ad-click forwarding) for use off-platform. Venues can alternatively upload their own custom PDF to override the generated one.
- **Ad Tracking** — connects a Meta Pixel ID and generates a per-venue "thank-you page" URL that fires the instant a pricing guide is downloaded, ready to paste into Meta as a Custom Conversion.
- **Booking-system dashboard** — the control center for the whole funnel above: toggle each Speed to Lead System stage on/off, see live lead counts sitting in each step, force-advance a stuck lead, send test messages, and reset any stage back to the default template — plus a listing analytics view (visitor traffic/geography, publish status) alongside the CRM pipeline value view in Marketing Analytics.

**Outcome:** A venue gets an additional, always-on discovery and lead-capture channel beyond its own website — every visitor from social, search, or the directory funnels into the same pricing guide → follow-up → booking flow, both owned and third-party reviews reinforce trust on the same page, and ad spend can be optimized against real, tracked conversions instead of guesswork.

**What it does:** Native, first-class integrations including:

- **Resend** — transactional and campaign email delivery.
- **DeepSeek (AI)** — powers the AI Concierge's conversational follow-up.
- **Google Calendar** — two-way sync that lets a venue link one or more calendars to block against for conflict-checking on tours and events.
- **Google Reviews** — connects the venue's Google Business Profile (by search, Maps URL, or Place ID) and auto-refreshes ratings/reviews onto the public listing every 24 hours.

- **Meta Pixel (Ad Tracking)** — connects a pixel ID plus a generated thank-you-page URL so every pricing-guide download fires as a trackable ad conversion.
- **Tripleseat** — pushes every new lead (name, email, phone, message, UTM source/medium/campaign) straight into Tripleseat the moment it's submitted, with a location selector for multi-location accounts and a one-click test-lead send.
- **Event Temple** — automatically creates a lead booking in Event Temple on form submission (contact details, wedding date, message, UTM attribution), with configurable pipeline/stage and referral-source mapping.
- **Calendly** — syncs upcoming scheduled events into the venue's calendar.
- **Zapier (native)** — a private API-key-based integration connecting StoryVenue to 6,000+ other apps without custom code.
- **HoneyBook (via Zapier)** — HoneyBook only grants direct API access to approved partners, so a guided Zapier recipe (StoryVenue "New Lead" trigger → HoneyBook "Create Client/Project" action) sends new leads over automatically, with nothing for the venue to apply for.

**Outcome:** A venue's existing CRM, ad platform, calendar, and review presence all stay in sync with StoryVenue automatically — no manual re-entry, no data stuck in one tool.

## PART 2 — BRIDE / COUPLE SIDE ("Wedding Planner")

### 1. Account & Profile

**What it does:** Couples create their own account and manage a shared profile: their name and their partner's name, wedding date and wedding time, guest count, mailing address, social links (Instagram/Facebook/TikTok/Pinterest), and password/email changes — all self-service.

**Outcome:** Every downstream feature (wedding website, calendar invites, RSVP counts, venue sync) pulls from one consistent, up-to-date profile the couple controls themselves.

---

### 2. Venue Discovery & Wish List

**What it does:** Couples can browse the public venue directory, save venues they're interested in to a personal wish list, and revisit saved venues (with live availability/publish status) later.

**Outcome:** Couples keep a running shortlist of venues they love in one place instead of screenshots and browser tabs, making it easy to compare and decide.

---

### 3. Connect With Your Venue (Wedding Planner linking)

**What it does:** Once booked, a couple searches for and requests to connect with her specific venue (or accepts an invite the venue already sent). Once connected:
- Her wedding date, guest count, assigned space, and coordinator info are shown to her automatically, pulled straight from the venue.
- Wedding date and guest count sync both directions in real time — if either the couple or the venue updates one, the other side updates automatically, so there's never a mismatch.
- A direct private message thread opens with the venue for planning questions.

**Outcome:** The couple and venue always see the exact same wedding date, guest count, and event details — no more "wait, I thought it was 120 guests" confusion — and planning conversations happen in one dedicated thread instead of scattered texts and emails.

---

### 4. Direct Messaging with the Venue

**What it does:** A real-time, threaded 1:1 conversation between the couple and their connected venue, with unread indicators and instant delivery.

**Outcome:** Every planning question and answer lives in one place both sides can always find again — nothing gets lost in a personal inbox or text thread.

---

### 5. Guest List & RSVP Management

**What it does:** Couples build and manage their full guest list: add guests individually (name, email, phone, address, party size, group/category), send digital invites by email with a personal RSVP link, or copy/share the link directly. A live summary shows total invited, attending, declined, and still-pending headcounts, plus a meal-choice breakdown across the whole guest list.
- **Per-guest meal & allergy tracking:** for parties with more than one person, each individual guest chooses their own meal and enters their own allergy/dietary notes — never a single shared choice forced on the whole party.
- **Email blasts:** the couple can send a custom email to her whole guest list, or filter to attending guests only, straight from the guest manager.
- Every guest gets a unique RSVP link/token so responses tie back to the right person automatically.

**Outcome:** The couple always has an accurate, real-time headcount and meal count without manually tallying replies, and every guest's dietary needs are captured individually and accurately for catering.

---

### 6. Seating & Room Layout

**What it does:** Two connected views:
- **Seating assignment:** assign each RSVP'd guest to a specific table, with live RSVP-status indicators (attending/pending/declined) so seating always reflects who's actually confirmed.
- **Room layout canvas:** a visual, drag-and-drop floor plan editor for arranging tables and room elements — the same shared layout the venue can see and collaborate on, saved conflict-safely so two people editing at once never overwrite each other's work.

**Outcome:** Couples plan their actual seating chart visually, exactly the way it will look in the room, and that plan is automatically visible to the venue — no separate spreadsheet or napkin sketch to hand over.

---

### 7. Wedding Website Builder ("Wedding Website")

**What it does:** A full self-service website/invitation editor with a live iPhone-accurate preview shown side-by-side as the couple edits, autosaving instantly (no save button — every change is confirmed live) with a two-column layout: editor on the left, phone preview on the right, always in sync pixel-for-pixel with what a guest actually sees.

Every section is independently editable, reorderable via drag-and-drop, and shown as a compact accordion when not being edited:

- **Cover photo & couple names/photo** — the top-of-page hero, shown first thing, not tucked in an accordion, so it always feels like the live preview of what guests see first.
- **Custom URL** — the couple picks their own web address for the site.
- **Settings (Live/Unpublish + Privacy)** — a single publish/unpublish toggle to take the site temporarily offline (with the countdown/status always visible), plus an optional private password so the invitation can be shared with only invited guests and no one else.
- **Story** — a rich-text "our story" section with a real formatting toolbar (bold, left/center/right justify, font size), so couples can style headers and body copy exactly how they want it, defaulting to left-justified.
- **Countdown** — a live countdown to the wedding date, always sized and boxed uniformly.
- **Photo gallery** — a Pinterest-style masonry photo gallery (mixed tall/wide images, tight uniform spacing, rounded corners) that always displays perfectly with no gaps or awkward whitespace regardless of the photos uploaded or the device viewing it. Clicking any photo opens a full-screen modal with left/right arrows to browse and a close button.
- **Venue & custom links** — the couple's booked venue is automatically shown first among the "custom links" section with its name and address, and tapping it opens directions in Google Maps; couples can add unlimited additional custom links (registry, hotel block, additional info, etc.) and drag them into any order.
- **Live stream / HTML embed** — an embed section with an explicit mode switch: either (a) a live-stream video embed that automatically takes over the cover photo the moment the event starts and reverts to the cover photo if the stream isn't live, with a tap-to-fullscreen control, or (b) a plain HTML embed block shown as a normal page section — the couple explicitly picks which behavior they want.
- **RSVP** — a floating "RSVP" button that's always visible at the bottom of the screen on both mobile and desktop, opening a centered pop-up modal (with a proper close button) where guests respond, choose per-person meals, and enter individual allergy notes.
- **Guestbook** — guests can leave notes that appear as iOS-style chat bubbles with a visible date/time stamp, animating in as visitors scroll so the wall feels alive in real time.
- **Add to calendar** — a one-tap button that opens the guest's own calendar app (Apple/Google/Outlook) directly, pre-filled with the wedding's date, time, title, and venue address — no downloading a file and re-opening it separately.

Every element on the page — gallery, links, text blocks, countdown, embed — can be dragged into whatever order the couple wants, with a live on-screen indicator showing exactly where an item will land while it's being dragged.

**Auto-close protection:** the site automatically unpublishes itself 30 days after the wedding date and releases its custom web address so no one is paying for or maintaining a stale page — the couple is shown a clear countdown/notice ahead of time and can restart/republish if they want to keep it live.

**Outcome:** Couples get a beautiful, fully custom wedding website with zero technical setup — no drag-and-drop website builder learning curve, no separate RSVP tool, no separate guestbook plugin — all built, previewed, and published from one screen that shows exactly what guests will see, in real time, with nothing to configure that could accidentally break the page on a guest's phone.

---

### 8. Public Wedding Website (guest-facing experience)

**What it does:** Every wedding gets its own public page at a couple-chosen web address, fully SEO/social-share optimized (proper page titles, descriptions, and preview images when shared in a text or on social media). Guests see the couple's cover photo, names, countdown, story, photo gallery, venue/links, optional live stream, and can:
- RSVP (with per-person meal and allergy choices) via a floating button always accessible on any device.
- Leave a note in the guestbook, timestamped and shown as it happens.
- Add the event straight to their own phone calendar in one tap.
- Unlock a password-protected invitation if the couple chose to keep it private.

**Outcome:** Every guest — regardless of device — gets a fast, polished, single-page experience for everything they need to know about the wedding, with no app download and no account required.

---

### 9. Data Consistency Across Bride & Venue

**What it does:** Wedding date and guest count are treated as a single source of truth once a couple links to her venue: whichever side updates the field first pushes the change to the other automatically, and a one-time reconciliation fills in any gap the moment the two connect (without ever overwriting a value either side already entered).

**Outcome:** The couple and the venue can never end up looking at two different wedding dates or guest counts — what one sees is always what the other sees.

---

## Summary

On the venue side, StoryVenue runs the full lifecycle of a booking — capture the lead, follow up automatically, book the tour, send the proposal, collect the signature and payment, and stay in sync with the couple all the way through the wedding and beyond — from one branded, integrated dashboard.

On the bride/couple side, StoryVenue gives every couple a self-service hub to manage their guest list, RSVPs, seating, and a fully custom wedding website, all directly connected to their actual venue — so both sides are always looking at the same live information.
